How we describe our own sites

Several of these properties carry the word "verified" in their domain name. On every one of them, the page itself states plainly what we do and do not check. We do not currently verify, rate, rank or endorse any supplier, consultant or business, we hold no review data, and we display no ratings.

Where a site lists a person or organisation, that entry is compiled from public information and is labelled as such. If we later introduce a verification process, each site will state exactly what evidence we hold before anything is described as verified.

How we work

  • The tools run in your browser. Where a site says your input never leaves your device, that is a description of how the code works, not a promise about our servers.
  • We cite sources and date them. On the reference guides, figures that change annually are marked as such and linked to where we got them.
  • We correct and remove on request. Every site carries a contact address for exactly this, and we action removal requests without argument.
  • We do not invent social proof. No fabricated reviews, ratings, testimonials, client counts or case studies appear on any property we run.
